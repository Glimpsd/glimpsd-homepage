Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Glimpsd (“Glimpsd,” “we,” “us”) is a location-based discovery platform that helps people find local places, events, Gems, and other people. This policy describes the personal information that may be collected when you use the website and related services, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

Depending on which features you use, information may include:

  • Account information: your name or display name, email address, account credentials or authentication identifiers, profile details, and preferences.
  • Location: an approximate location inferred from your IP address or precise location you choose to share through device permissions, used to display nearby Places, Events, People, and Gems.
  • User content: business or event listings, Gems, descriptions, photos, messages, reports, and other submissions.
  • Usage and technical information: device and browser information, IP address, logs, pages visited, referral data, and performance or anti-abuse signals.
  • Transactions: business subscription status and purchase-related records, where a paid feature is offered. Payment details may be processed by a payment provider rather than by Glimpsd.

2. Identity verification with Didit

To help establish authentic accounts and prevent impersonation or abuse, Glimpsd may require identity verification through Didit, a third-party identity-verification provider. Depending on the verification method, Didit may ask for a government-issued identification document, a selfie, a liveness capture, and related device or fraud-prevention information. This may involve biometric information, such as a facial template, subject to applicable law and the notices and consent presented at verification.

Didit processes verification information on behalf of the organization requesting verification, subject to its service arrangements and legal obligations. Glimpsd may receive verification status, results, risk signals, or other session details depending on the integration. We use the information we receive to determine account eligibility, protect the platform, and investigate fraud or misuse. We do not describe identity-verification records or images as never accessible to Glimpsd because this depends on the actual integration configuration.

Before completing a verification, please read Didit's Verification Privacy Notice, Biometric Data Retention and Destruction Policy, and End User Terms. Glimpsd must configure verification retention appropriately and provide any legally required biometric-data notice or consent before capture. Storage periods depend on configured settings, the type of data, and applicable law.

3. How we use information

  • Operate and personalize nearby discovery, maps, events, business pages, and Gems.
  • Enable account access, optional social connections, messages, and provider submissions.
  • Verify account authenticity, prevent fraud, enforce policies, and respond to reports.
  • Maintain the service, diagnose errors, and understand product performance.
  • Manage subscriptions and communications about the service.

4. Sharing and visibility

Listings and content you choose to make public may be visible to other people and search engines. Location shared through public posts or event listings may reveal where an activity occurs. We may share information with hosting, analytics, mapping, payment, identity-verification, security, and customer-support providers as needed to operate the service, under applicable agreements. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

5. Location choices

You can manage precise-location permission in your browser or device settings. If you decline, Glimpsd may offer limited discovery based on an approximate area or a place you select. Public business and event locations are not treated as private personal location data.

6. Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for login sessions, preferences, security, and measurement. Where legally required, we will provide applicable choices or consent controls. Traffic and engagement metrics may be filtered to reduce automated or crawler activity, but no method is perfect.

7. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and address security concerns. Retention depends on data type and system settings; a universal retention period is not promised here. Didit verification records and any biometric information are also governed by Didit's service configuration and applicable retention/deletion requirements. We use safeguards designed to protect information, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your privacy choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law. You can also manage location and other device permissions. For identity-verification data controlled by Glimpsd, contact Glimpsd first; requests relating to Didit may also be directed to privacy@didit.me. Certain records may need to be retained for lawful purposes.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Account age requirements, if any, are communicated during registration.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as features, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above indicates the current version.

11. Contact

For questions or requests about Glimpsd's privacy practices, contact privacy@glimpsd.com.
